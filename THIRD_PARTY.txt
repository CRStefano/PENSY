PENSY includes these third-party components, all used offline on the phone.

ZXing core 3.5 (QR codes and barcodes)
  https://github.com/zxing/zxing
  Apache License 2.0

Tesseract OCR 5.5.1 (text recognition), compiled into libpensyocr.so
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract
  Apache License 2.0

Leptonica 1.85.0 (image processing used by Tesseract), compiled into libpensyocr.so
  https://github.com/DanBloomberg/leptonica
  BSD 2-Clause style license (leptonica-license.txt in its sources)

tessdata_fast, Italian and English models ita.traineddata and eng.traineddata
(android/app/src/main/assets/tessdata)
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
  Apache License 2.0

Material Icons (vector paths in android/app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_*.xml)
  https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
  Apache License 2.0

Kotlin standard library
  https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin
  Apache License 2.0
