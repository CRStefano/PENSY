## Welcome to PENSY
PENSY keeps your everyday papers in order and tells you what to do and when. This manual explains every part of the app: what it does, how to use it, what it is for and why it works the way it does.

The idea is a single one: photo, card, calendar, how much is left. You take a photo of a bill or upload the PDF you were sent; PENSY reads the amount and the due date, files it in the right folder, reminds you in time and counts it in the month's budget. Around this idea come health, everyday spending, savings and investments, and the people in your family.

### How it is organised
At the bottom there are five tabs, always the same:
- Today: how much is left this month, what is due, today's medicines.
- Folders: the drawers where documents live, including the Health record.
- Calendar: every deadline, day by day.
- Budget: income, expenses, spending, savings and investments.
- Settings: people, security, reminders, the data folder, this manual.

The round «Add» button, bottom right, is the quickest way to put something in: a document, a payslip, a spending, a medicine, a visit.

### How to read this manual
Each chapter stands on its own. The «Tip» boxes give you a practical trick; the «Why it works this way» boxes explain the choices behind a feature, so you know what to expect. You can search for a word at the top and save the whole manual as a PDF.

> 💡 **Tip:** if you only have five minutes, read «First steps» and «Adding a document». You will discover the rest by using the app.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** PENSY was made for people who have no time to keep an archive. So it does everything it can by itself (reading, filing, reminding, calculating) and only asks you to confirm.

## First steps
The five things that make PENSY useful from day one. On Today you find them in the «First steps» card, which ticks itself.

### The first launch
At the first launch PENSY asks three things, and each one can be skipped:
1. The language and your name.
2. Who you use it for: just you, also a son or daughter, or a parent. Each person will have their own folders and health record.
3. Where to save your data: a folder on the phone where PENSY keeps an encrypted copy, PENSY_DATA.

If you have used PENSY on another phone, tap «Restore my data» and choose the PENSY_DATA folder or the .pensy file: everything comes back.

### The first steps
- Add your first document: a bill, a pagoPA notice, a medical report. With a photo or by uploading the file.
- Add your payslip: from it PENSY works out how much is left each month. The PDF that came by email is fine too.
- Choose where to save your data, with a password you pick.
- Turn on notifications: without them, PENSY cannot remind you.
- Look at the Health record: medicines, visits, allergies.

When you have done everything the card disappears. If something is not for you (for example you have no payslip), tap «I don't need it»; to hide the card straight away, tap «Hide».

> 💡 **Tip:** the first time you open Folders, Calendar or Budget you see a one-line hint. Close it with the ✕; to see them all again, Settings › «Show the hints again».

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** instead of a tour to watch, the first steps are things to do, each with its own button. You learn by doing, and after a week the app is already full of your own things.

## Adding a document
Tap «Add» and choose a photo or a file. PENSY reads the text, shows you what it understood and you confirm.

### Photo or file
Everywhere in PENSY, when you add a document, you can choose:
- Take a photo: for paper sheets.
- Upload a file (PDF or image): for what reaches you by email, from a website or a chat. You can pick several images together: they become the pages of one document. A PDF is read up to 12 pages.

This works for payslips, the report of a visit, the receipt of a payment and pages added later too.

### From your email or WhatsApp
No need to save the attachment first. In your email app tap the attachment and choose «Share» › PENSY, or «Open with» › PENSY for PDFs. From WhatsApp or the gallery you can share several photos at once.

### What PENSY reads
The kind of document, who sent it, the amount, the due date, the pagoPA notice code, the issuer's tax code and the exact content of QR codes and barcodes. In medical reports it finds the check-ups to book («check-up in 12 months»); in prescriptions the NRE number; in identity documents the expiry date.

### Checking and saving
Before saving you see a card with everything PENSY understood. You can correct the kind, issuer, amount, due date, how often it repeats, when to remind you and the folder. The text read stays below, in «Document text». If the document has several pages, tap «Add a page» before saving.

After saving, a line at the bottom tells you where it went and when the reminder will come, with «See» to open the folder.

### Entering by hand
For something without paper (an instalment, a reminder) choose «Add» › «Enter by hand».

> 💡 **Tip:** for a good photo lay the sheet on a dark surface, with plenty of light and no shadows, and hold the phone parallel. If there is a QR code, make sure it is clearly visible.

> 💡 **Tip:** for identity cards, warranties and receipts turn on «Keep the photo» before saving.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** usually the photo is deleted after reading, and the text and the codes remain; PENSY redraws the codes exactly. This keeps the app light and fast, and a photo of your document does not stay around on the phone unless you want it.

## Today
The first screen: how much is left this month and what needs doing now.

### What you find
- At the top the greeting, the search across all documents, the question mark for help and the circle of the person in use.
- The coloured card: how much is left this month, with income, expenses and what is still to pay. From here you add a payslip or a spending on the fly.
- «Heavy month»: a warning when a big expense lands together with others, also in the next two months.
- The deadlines of the other people you look after, if any.
- Today's health: the medicine doses to mark, medicines to reorder, visits coming up.
- Overdue, due in the next 45 days, and to pay with no due date.

> 💡 **Tip:** tap a deadline to see the QR code to show at the counter or to mark it as paid.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** the question people actually ask is «how much do I have left?», not «how much did I spend?». That is why the big number is what is left, after the month's deadlines, spending, savings and investments.

## Folders
PENSY's drawers: Home, Car, Health, Pets, School and university, Work, Taxes and documents, Other. Every document goes into the right one by itself.

### How they work
Open a folder to see the things to do, the subfolders and the documents. You can create subfolders at any depth: Health › Heart, Car › Panda, Home › Mortgage.

PENSY picks the folder from the kind of document and the words it contains. If you correct it, PENSY remembers: later documents from the same issuer go where you put them.

### The summary
At the top of each folder there is a note always in view: the plate and policy number in Car, the microchip in Pets, the family doctor in Health.

### Tools
- Folder as PDF: the icon at the top makes a PDF with the things to do and every document, to send or print.
- Car reminders: in Car enter the dates you know and PENSY creates the inspection, insurance, road tax, service and tyre changes.
- Pet reminders: in Pets, vaccine, antiparasitic treatment, yearly visit and weight.
- Search all documents: searches the text, titles, issuers and notes.

> 💡 **Tip:** deleting a folder loses nothing: its documents and deadlines move to the folder above or to «Other».

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** people do not think in kinds of documents but in areas of life (the home, the car, health). Folders follow that way of thinking.

## Deadlines and reminders
Everything to pay or do by a date becomes a deadline, with its reminders.

### When reminders come
For every unpaid deadline PENSY reminds you a few days before (10 days, unless you choose otherwise), the day before and on the day, at the time you choose in Settings. If you choose a long notice, it adds a reminder one week before (beyond two weeks) and one month before (beyond a month). If it is still unpaid, a last reminder comes when it is overdue.

Some documents have their own notice already: identity documents 90 days before, warranties, policies and subscriptions 30 days, prescriptions 7 days.

### Things that repeat
Set «Repeats» (every month, every 2 months, every year…). PENSY forecasts the next ones with the last amount and, when the real one arrives, updates the forecast instead of making a duplicate. When you mark a deadline as paid, it creates the next one straight away.

### Paying and keeping the receipt
Open the deadline: the QR code or barcode is drawn exactly as the original; tap it to see it large. Then «Mark as paid». With «Attach the receipt» you take a photo of the bank receipt or upload it: it stays linked to the payment.

### Documents, warranties, subscriptions
- Identity card, driving licence, passport: PENSY reads the expiry date or works it out from the issue date.
- Warranties: the warranty ends two years after the purchase, or as written.
- Subscriptions and contracts: fill in «Cancel by» and PENSY reminds you in time to cancel.

### No due date
If something has no due date, turn on «No due date»: you will find it on Today, among the things to pay with no due date.

> 💡 **Tip:** if a reminder does not arrive, check in the phone's settings that PENSY's notifications are on and that PENSY is not among the battery-optimised apps.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** a single reminder is easily forgotten. Several reminders, always at the same time, give you time to get organised without flooding you with notifications.

## Calendar
Every deadline of the month, day by day, with the amounts.

### How to read it
Each day with something to do has a dot: purple to pay, green paid, red overdue, grey forecast. A second dot marks health: visits, planned operations, medicines to reorder and the medicines you don't take every day. Tap a day, red ones too: right under the calendar you see only what is on that day; «Whole month» goes back to the full list. The visits and operations of the other people you look after are there too, with their name in front.

> 💡 **Tip:** use the arrows to look at the coming months: repeating expenses are already there, forecast.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** the calendar is not a diary to fill in: it fills itself with what you add elsewhere.

## Budget
Income, expenses and what is left, month by month, past and forecast.

### The payslip
Add it with a photo or with the PDF that came by email: PENSY reads the net pay and the month. If a month is missing, it uses the last regular payslip as an estimate (without the extra months). You can mark an extra month as such.

### Other income
Money that comes in outside the payslip: a friend paying you back for dinner, a gift, something you sold, a small job, a refund. In «Budget» › «Add income», or «Add» › «New income»: amount, where it comes from, save. It adds to the month's income and does not change the salary estimate: if this month's payslip is not there yet, PENSY keeps using the last one as an estimate and adds this on top.

### The chart
Twelve bars: five months back, the current one and six ahead. The bars are the expenses, the green line the income, future months are lighter. Tap a bar to see that month.

### What counts as expenses
The month's deadlines, everyday spending, what you put in the jars and what you put into investments (including future savings plan contributions). So the number that is left is really yours to spend.

### Everyday spending
«Spending» on Today, or «Add» › «New spending»: amount, category, save. In the budget you see them by category.

### Card or cash
Every expense and every income says how the money moved: «Card or account» or «Cash». PENSY remembers your last choice, so usually you don't have to touch anything. In the month's budget you see how much went by card and how much in cash. It matters for the 730 too: most deductions need a traceable payment, and PENSY leaves out by itself the cash expenses that don't count.

### More
- Fixed costs: what subscriptions, contracts, bills and instalments cost you in a year.
- Comparison with the previous month, in percent.
- Export: deadlines, spending and income as CSV for Excel.
- Trends: the months put together (next chapter).

> 💡 **Tip:** record small expenses as soon as you make them: it takes three taps, and at the end of the month you will not wonder where the money went.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** a useful budget looks ahead. Knowing in October that December will be heavy gives you two months to prepare.

> 🧭 **Why there are no two wallets:** keeping two separate balances, cash and card, would mean recording every cash withdrawal too and starting from an initial balance; one forgotten expense and the numbers no longer match your wallet nor your bank app, which already has the real balance. PENSY's budget answers a different question: how much you have left to spend this month.

## Trends
The months put together: how income and expenses went over six months, a year or all time.

### What it shows
- The period: 6 months, 12 months, the current year or all.
- The period's totals and the monthly averages: income, expenses, put aside, how much was left on average.
- The chart of income and expenses, month by month (or year by year over long periods). Tap a column to see the numbers.
- Net pay over time: raises, extra months, how much it changed since the start of the period.
- Everyday spending by category, added up over the whole period, with the monthly average.
- Year by year: income, expenses and what was left.

Open it from Budget, with the «Trends» button under the chart.

> 💡 **Tip:** look at the categories over 12 months: small, frequent expenses (cafés, deliveries) often add up to a surprise.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** only what was actually recorded counts here, with no estimates or forecasts. It is a mirror of how things went, not a guess.

## Savings and investments
Everything you have put aside: jars and investments, with charts and projections.

### Jars
For putting money aside for something: holidays, the car, presents. Write how much and by when: PENSY tells you how much to put aside each month and takes it off what is left. The money stays where it is: the jar keeps count of it.

### Investments
An ETF, shares, a pension fund, a deposit account, postal savings bonds. For each one:
1. Give it a name and pick the kind; if you like, add the ISIN or ticker.
2. Write the average yearly return you expect: it is only used for projections.
3. If you put in a fixed amount (for example 100 € every month on the 5th), set the savings plan: PENSY records each contribution by itself when its day comes and counts it in the budget. If the plan started in the past, it also records the contributions already made.
4. Add the purchases you make by hand, with the units if you know them.
5. Every now and then tap «Update the value» and copy from your bank or broker app the total value, or the price of one unit.

### What you see
- How much you put in, what it is worth, the gain in euro and in percent.
- How it went: put in and value, month by month.
- In a few years: at 5, 10, 20 or 30 years, put in against estimated value, with the returns.
- How the total is split among your investments.

### The savings calculator
Try before you start: starting amount, monthly amount, average return and years. You immediately see how much you will have put in and what it could be worth.

> 💡 **Tip:** use a prudent return for projections. Better a nice surprise than a disappointment.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** PENSY does not use the internet, so it does not download prices. You update them whenever you like: a few seconds a month, and nobody knows what you own. Projections are estimates, not advice: past returns do not guarantee future ones.

## The 730 and exports
The year's expenses that may lower your taxes, ready for the CAF (Italian tax assistance centre).

### How it works
When you record a spending choose «For the 730»: medical expenses, vet, school, university, public transport, children's sport. Medical tickets and university fees count by themselves. In Budget › «Expenses for the tax return (730)» you see the year's total by kind, an estimate of what you get back and a CSV for the CAF.

Many deductions require a traceable payment: mark when you paid in cash and PENSY reminds you.

### Exporting to CSV
In Budget, the share icon at the top makes a CSV of deadlines or spending, which opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

> 💡 **Tip:** keep the receipt showing your tax code: take a photo and turn on «Keep the photo».

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** it is an estimate so you don't arrive unprepared, not the tax return itself. You file that with the CAF or the pre-filled return.

## Health
In the Health folder: conditions, allergies, medicines, visits, measurements, reports. Everything ready when you see the doctor.

### Medicines
Add the medicine with the dose (tablets, drops, sachets, syringes…), when you take it and, if you like, an end date. «When» can be:
- every day, every N days or every N weeks (for example every 4 weeks);
- some days of the week (for example Monday and Thursday);
- every month on a given day (the 15th) or on a day of the week (the first Monday), also every 2, 3 or 6 months;
- just once, on a given day;
- when needed.

The time is optional. With a time, the reminder rings then; without a time the dose shows on Today as «during the day» and PENSY reminds you at the reminder time. Mark the dose as taken or skipped, also from the notification («Taken», «Skip», «In 10 minutes»). In the medicine you see the next time and how the last 30 days went. Medicines you don't take every day also show in the calendar.

In Settings you can make medicine reminders ring like an alarm, even in silent mode.

### Stock
- Count the boxes: write how many boxes you have, how many doses are in a box and how many boxes you want to keep aside. When only those are left, PENSY reminds you to order more. When you buy, tap «I bought a pack».
- Count one by one: write how many tablets (or drops, syringes…) you have; PENSY works out when they run out and reminds you a few days before.
- I order at fixed intervals: if you always order it after the same number of days.

### Visits
To book (perhaps by a date, with the prescription's priority), booked (with day, time and place) and done (with the report). PENSY reminds you beforehand. Check-ups found in reports become visits to book.

### Measurements
Blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, height, temperature, oxygen saturation, heart rate, with the chart, minimum, average and maximum, and CSV export. With weight and height PENSY also shows the body mass index, as a number: what it means is for the doctor to say.

### At the doctor's
«Show to the doctor» makes a clean page with conditions, allergies, medicines with doses and times, vaccines and visits. With «Share as PDF» you add the measurement charts and how the medicines were taken.

### Emergency, vaccines, prescriptions
- Conditions and allergies: «Since when», or «Since birth».
- Operations: if one is planned, add it with its date: it goes in the calendar and PENSY reminds you a week before, the day before and on the day.
- Emergency card: blood group, allergies, medicines and who to call, also on the lock screen if you want.
- Vaccines with boosters: PENSY reminds you a month before.
- Electronic prescription: PENSY reads the NRE and reminds you that a prescription for medicines is valid for 30 days; «Show to the pharmacist» shows it large.

> 💡 **Tip:** for a pet create a subfolder in Pets (Pets › Rex): medicines, visits and weight work the same way.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** at the doctor's you always forget something. One page with everything, in large print, saves time for you and for them.

## People and family
You can keep your children's or a parent's papers too, each with their own things.

### How it works
Tap the circle with the initial at the top of Today to switch between people or add one. Each person has their own folders, documents, deadlines, health record, jars and investments. Reminders come for everyone, with the name in front.

For a child you can choose that their expenses count in your budget: in yours you see theirs too, in theirs only their own. On Today a line reminds you of the other people's upcoming deadlines.

> 💡 **Tip:** when a child grows up, their person can have a budget of their own: change it in Settings › People.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** in a family it is usually one person who keeps everyone's papers. With separate people nothing gets mixed up, yet everything is in one place.

## Your data
Your data lives only on your phone. The PENSY_DATA folder is your backup.

### The PENSY_DATA folder
Choose a folder on the phone: every time you change something PENSY saves an encrypted copy of everything there, with a password you pick. If you keep document photos, they go there too, encrypted. Every day PENSY keeps a copy of how the data was, for the last 7 days.

### Changing phone
1. Copy the PENSY_DATA folder to the new phone (cable, USB stick or the service you prefer).
2. Install PENSY and at the first launch tap «Restore my data», or Settings › «Import data from another phone».
3. Choose the folder and type the password.

Without cables: Settings › «Send to another phone» makes a single encrypted file and opens Android's share sheet (Quick Share, Bluetooth).

### Going back
Deleted something by mistake? Settings › «Restore a copy», choose the day and type the password.

> 💡 **Tip:** write the password down somewhere safe. If you lose it, nobody can recover the data in the folder, not even us.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** no server and no account means nobody but you can see your papers. The encrypted copy in a folder of yours gives you the safety of a backup without handing your data to anyone.

## Security and privacy
Your papers are personal: PENSY protects them on the phone too.

### The lock
Settings › Privacy › «Lock PENSY with fingerprint or PIN». PENSY opens with the phone's fingerprint, face or PIN, at start and when you come back after a minute. In recent apps the content stays hidden. PENSY does not know your fingerprint: it asks Android.

### Other protections
- Notifications on the lock screen show no amounts or names.
- With the lock on, the widget only shows how many deadlines there are.
- PENSY has no permission to use the internet: Android itself prevents it.

> 💡 **Tip:** the lock needs a screen lock on the phone. If you have none, set it up first in the phone's settings.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** an app with identity documents, medical reports and accounts must be safe even if you lend your phone to someone for a minute.

## Widget, notifications and languages
The everyday conveniences.

### The widget
Long-press the Home screen, choose «Widgets» and look for PENSY. It shows the next three deadlines and today's medicines; the camera button lets you add a document straight away, with a photo or a file.

### Notifications
In Settings › Reminders choose how many days before to remind you and at what time. For medicines at the exact time PENSY needs the «Alarms & reminders» permission, which it asks for when needed.

### Languages
The app is in Italian and English: Settings › Language. In «Language of documents» choose «Italian and English» if you receive bills or invoices in English; reading becomes a little slower.

> 💡 **Tip:** if the phone closes apps to save battery, remove PENSY from the optimisations: reminders will arrive on time.

> 🧭 **Why it works this way:** notifications are few and always at the same time. An app that disturbs often ends up ignored.

## Why PENSY works this way
The choices behind the whole app.

### Truly offline
PENSY has no permission to connect to the internet. It is not a promise: Android itself prevents it. No server, no account, no ads, no data sold.

### Light and fast
PENSY is written without heavy libraries and reads the text of documents right on the phone. It opens in a moment and takes little space.

### Yours
The data is in a folder of yours, encrypted with your password. You can take it to another phone, keep a copy wherever you like, export what you need as CSV or PDF.

### It does the work, you confirm
Reading, filing, reminding, calculating: PENSY tries to do it all by itself and only asks you to check. When it gets something wrong, it learns from your corrections.

> 💡 **Tip:** if the manual does not answer a question, look at the frequently asked questions: the question mark on Today, or Settings › Help.
